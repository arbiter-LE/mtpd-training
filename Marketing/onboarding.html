What happens next

  1. 1
    Send your files
    Email your roster spreadsheet and badge image to andrew@arbiterle.com. If you didn't share a link to your policy manual, attach your policy documents too.
  2. 2
    Agreement
    We'll send your service agreement directly for review and signature. Setup begins once it's signed.
  3. 3
    Platform configuration
    We'll configure your subdomain, import your roster, and assign admin accounts.
  4. 4
    Onboarding call
    30-minute walkthrough with your admin contacts before go-live.
  5. 5
    Go live
    Officers receive credentials. Training begins on your requested date.
