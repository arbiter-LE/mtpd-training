Why it matters

Inconsistent training is a liability problem.

When an officer is involved in a use-of-force incident, a pursuit, or a domestic violence response, one of the first questions asked is:

What was this officer trained to do, and can you prove it?

If the answer is "we trained everyone, we just can't document it," that's exposure.

Small departments face this disproportionately. Without the budget for regular scenario-based training, officers learn from each other, from experience, and from whatever resources happen to be available. The standard varies officer to officer. So does the risk.

Arbiter LE gives every officer in your department the same scenario-driven training, and gives command staff the records to prove it happened.

How it works

Your policy, applied in context.

Most online training is a generic course catalog. Arbiter LE puts your own SOPs and general orders inside the situations officers actually face: use of force, search and seizure, domestic violence response, pursuits, crisis intervention, and more.

  1. Step 1

    Built from your orders

    Your general orders appear in each module word for word, never reworded or simplified. Scenarios use your jurisdiction's real roads and conditions.

  2. Step 2

    Trained in short sessions

    Officers make decisions in a realistic scenario, then answer a knowledge check drawn from the module. The goal is under 15 minutes a week, on any phone, tablet, or desktop.

  3. Step 3

    Documented automatically

    Every completion, score, and attempt is recorded as it happens. Command staff see the whole roster and can open any officer's full record.

About

Built from inside the profession.

Arbiter LE was built by an active municipal police officer with over 14 years on the job, including time as a Field Training Officer preparing new officers to do the work right.

The problem was simple and frustrating. Small departments want to train their people to a high standard, but scenario-based training is expensive. Outside instructors, facility time, and scheduling across shifts add up fast, and most small departments don't have the budget. So training becomes inconsistent. Some officers get it, some don't, and nobody can prove who was trained on what, or when.

Arbiter LE exists to close that gap: professional, scenario-driven training that small departments can afford, with the documentation to back it up.

Questions

What chiefs ask first.

How accurate is the policy and legal content?

Your department's general orders and SOPs appear verbatim — never reworded, simplified, or paraphrased. Scenarios use your jurisdiction's real roads and conditions. Any court case referenced is verified against the actual ruling before it ever reaches an officer.

What happens to my officers' data, and who can see it?

Your department runs on its own private, isolated database — your data never mixes with another agency's. Each officer sees only their own record; command staff see only their own department. Scenario choices used for Decision Insights are recorded without officer names or badges, and command staff see only department-wide percentages. Training records are never sold, shared, or used to train any outside system or AI.

Will my policies or data be shared with other agencies?

No. Every department is fully walled off. Your SOPs are used only to build your department's training, and nothing crosses between agencies. Each engagement stands entirely on its own.

How do I know officers actually completed the training?

Every module pairs decision-based scenarios with a knowledge check drawn directly from that module's material. The command dashboard tracks completions, scores, and attempts automatically — giving you a clear, defensible record of who was trained on what, and when. For accreditation files, a training evidence packet organizes those records by your own general orders, with officer transcripts and attestation lines.

Does this replace our own policies or training?

No — it reinforces them. Arbiter LE helps officers engage with and retain the policies you already have in place. It never replaces your department's orders or an officer's judgment, and it never invents policy.

Who builds the platform?

Arbiter LE is built from inside the profession — by an active law enforcement officer who has worked the road under the same kind of policies your officers do. That perspective is built into every scenario.

How does pricing work?

Straightforward and transparent: a flat annual rate based on your department's size, billed annually. No setup fees, no free trials, no surprises. Reach out and we'll provide a clear quote specific to your agency.
